## 📚 ARC-AGI-3 — Research & Further Reading

*ARC-AGI-3 explores how intelligent agents learn to navigate unfamiliar interactive environments, discover hidden rules, and solve multi-step challenges efficiently. The following resources provide useful background for understanding the benchmark and its research goals.*

| **Resource** | **What we can learn and why it matters** |
| --- | --- |
| [ARC-AGI-3 — Official Website](https://arcprize.org/arc-agi/3) | An introduction to the benchmark, its objectives, and why adapting to unfamiliar environments is a fundamental challenge for AI. |
| [ARC-AGI-3 — Technical Report (PDF)](https://arcprize.org/media/ARC_AGI_3_Technical_Report.pdf) | The benchmark's design, interactive environments, evaluation methodology, and action-efficiency scoring. |
| [ARC-AGI-3 — Interactive Games](https://arc3.games/) | Experience the games firsthand and explore the challenges agents face when discovering controls, objectives, and hidden mechanics. |
| [Measuring Human Performance on ARC-AGI-3](https://arcprize.org/blog/arc-agi-3-human-dataset) | Human gameplay trajectories that reveal how people explore unfamiliar environments, test hypotheses, learn rules, and improve their strategies. |
| [Tufa Labs — Duck Harness](https://github.com/Tufalabs/duck-harness) | The open-source agent framework underlying this work, including its reasoning loop, interactive tools, gameplay observations, and diagnostics. |

*These resources provide scientific and technical context for our ongoing investigation into agent reasoning, exploration, memory, planning, and adaptation to unseen environments. They are references for further study, not claims that additional methods have already been implemented in this notebook.*
